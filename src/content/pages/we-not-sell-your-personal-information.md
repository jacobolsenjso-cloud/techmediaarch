<p>Tech Media Arch does not sell personal information for money, and does not collect names, addresses or other contact details from readers.</p>
<h2>Ads and "sharing"</h2>
<p>The site shows ads from Google AdSense and measures visits with Google Analytics. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your interests. Under some U.S. state privacy laws, such as California's, this kind of advertising can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, even when no money changes hands for the data itself. What is processed is described in our <a href="/p/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
<h2>How to opt out</h2>
<ul>
<li>Use the "Privacy and cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, where it is shown, to change your choice for Google's ads and analytics.</li>
<li>Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.</li>
<li>Or write to <a href="mailto:info@techmediaarch.com">info@techmediaarch.com</a>, and we will help you.</li>
</ul>
<p>Visitors in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any analytics or advertising cookies are used.</p>
