<p><strong>Tech Media Arch is the checked tech answer archive.</strong> It collects short, sourced answers to the tech questions people actually ask, and keeps them in one place you can come back to. It is independent and run by Jacob S. Olsen from Denmark.</p>
<h2>How an answer works</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>The question is the title</strong>, so you can see at once whether the page answers what you were looking for.</li>
<li><strong>The short answer comes first.</strong> The details, examples and a FAQ follow for those who want more.</li>
<li><strong>Named sources you can check</strong> are listed at the end, and a verification record shows when the answer was published, updated and how it was written.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Who writes the answers</h2>
<p>For the older articles, Jacob chose each subject and the search phrases it answers. Since late September 2026, most new answers are written by an AI language model that researches each question on the web, names its sources and is only published if at least two sources check out. They are published automatically, and Jacob does not read every answer before it goes live. What is his is the machinery and the rules it follows. Read exactly how it works in our <a href="/p/editorial-guidelines">Editorial Guidelines</a>.</p>
