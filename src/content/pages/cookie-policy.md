<p><strong>Last updated: 2 November 2026</strong></p>
<p>Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Tech Media Arch uses as few as it can. The full picture of what data is processed is in our <a href="/p/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
<h2>What is used</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Your consent choice.</strong> Google's consent message stores your choice, so you are not asked on every page.</li>
<li><strong>Security.</strong> Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may use short-lived technical data to tell people from automated traffic.</li>
<li><strong>Statistics.</strong> Google Analytics cookies count visits and pages read. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are only used if you agree.</li>
<li><strong>Ads.</strong> Google AdSense cookies show and measure ads, personalised only if you agree (in the regions above).</li>
<li><strong>YouTube.</strong> If you click play on a video in an older article, YouTube may set its own cookies.</li>
<li><strong>Theme.</strong> Your choice of light or dark mode is saved in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Change your choice</h2>
<p>Use the "Privacy and cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page to change or withdraw your consent. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site still works, but ads may be less relevant.</p>
