<p>An AI is watching me through my webcam right now. Every few seconds, it checks my posture. If I start slouching, I get a notification on my Mac telling me to sit up straight. If you tried this with a service like ChatGPT, you'd be sending thousands of images of yourself to a server and paying a small fortune for it. But my setup is completely free and 100% private. Every photo stays on my computer. This is the power of local AI, and today I'm showing you how to build it step-by-step, even if you've never written a line of code.</p><div data-youtube-video=""><iframe allowfullscreen="true" autoplay="false" disablekbcontrols="false" enableiframeapi="false" endtime="0" height="480" ivloadpolicy="0" loop="false" modestbranding="false" origin="" playlist="" rel="1" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Tx4HUJJdYxA?rel=1" start="0" width="640"></iframe></div><h2>Key Takeaways</h2><ul><li><p>You can run powerful AI models like Google's Gemma 4 on your own computer, completely free of charge.</p></li><li><p>Local AI guarantees your privacy because your data, like webcam images, never leaves your machine.</p></li><li><p>You don't need to be a coder; you can ask the AI to write the necessary scripts for you.</p></li><li><p>This project opens the door to building all sorts of custom, private AI tools.</p></li></ul><h2>Why Local AI is a Game-Changer</h2><p>Cloud-based AI is amazing, but it has two big drawbacks: cost and privacy. Sending a constant stream of images to an online service for analysis would get expensive fast. More importantly, do you really want a company storing thousands of pictures of you at your desk?</p><p>This is where local AI changes everything. Google's new Gemma 4 model is a powerful tool that you can download and run directly on your own machine. It's part of a trend where <a href="/2026/04/jitro-explained-why-googles-latest-ai-is-raising-alarms.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">artificial intelligence is moving faster than anyone expected</a>, putting incredible capabilities into everyone's hands. Your data stays with you, and the cost is zero.</p><h2>Getting Your Gear Ready</h2><p>You don't need a supercomputer for this. The lightweight version of Gemma 4 can run on most modern machines.</p><h3>Hardware You'll Need</h3><p>I'm using a Mac with an M3 chip, but this will work on any Mac with over 8GB of RAM. For Windows or Linux users, a recent Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 should handle it just fine. If you have a dedicated graphics card, things will run noticeably faster. For Mac users looking for a graphics boost, exploring options like <a href="/2026/04/nvidia-egpus-and-mac.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Nvidia eGPUs can unlock new possibilities</a>.</p><h3>The Software</h3><p>We need two free, open-source tools:</p><ol><li><p><strong>Ollama:</strong> This is a simple program that lets you download and run AI models like Gemma on your computer. No accounts, no API keys.</p></li><li><p><strong>Gemma 4 (E4B model):</strong> This is the "brain" of our operation. It's a lightweight but capable vision model from Google that will analyze your posture.</p></li></ol><h2>Step-by-Step: Building Your AI Posture Coach</h2><p>Ready to build? This is easier than you think. Just open the Terminal app on your Mac and follow along.</p><h3>Step 1: Install Ollama</h3><p>First, we need to get Ollama on your machine. It's just one command you copy and paste into your terminal. In seconds, the framework for running your local AI will be installed.</p><h3>Step 2: Download the Gemma 4 Model</h3><p>Next, we'll download the AI model itself. With Ollama installed, you just run another simple command to pull the Gemma 4 E4B model onto your computer. That's it. You now have a powerful Google AI living on your hard drive.</p><h3>Step 3: Let the AI Write the Code</h3><p>This is where the magic happens. I have no idea how to write a script that connects my webcam to an AI. So, I just asked Gemma.</p><p>I started the model through Ollama and gave it a plain English prompt: "Give me a Python script that captures a frame using my webcam, sends it to Gemma 4 to analyze my posture, and sends me a message back."</p><p>To my surprise, the lightweight model spit out a working script. It even told me what other small things I needed to install first. This is a perfect example of how you can use AI to <a href="/2026/04/mastering-chatgpt.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">develop effective workflow strategies</a> and build things you never thought you could.</p><h3>Step 4: Run the Script and Get Feedback</h3><p>I saved the code as a Python file and ran it from the terminal. Immediately, it started using my webcam to check my posture every five seconds and told me I was slouching. It worked!</p><h2>Making Your AI Coach Even Smarter</h2><p>The basic script was great, but it could be better. So, I went back to my new AI coding assistant.</p><h3>From Terminal to Desktop Notifications</h3><p>Staring at a terminal window all day isn't practical. I asked Gemma to upgrade the script to send me an actual Mac notification when I'm slouching. Now, no matter what I'm doing, a small pop-up appears in the corner to remind me.</p><h3>Reducing Notification Spam</h3><p>The constant "Slouching!" notifications got annoying fast. So, I asked for one more upgrade: "Only notify me if I've been slouching consistently, like in eight of the last ten checks." This made the alerts much more meaningful and less distracting.</p><h2>Beyond Posture: What Else Can You Build?</h2><p>A posture coach is just the beginning. Once you have a private vision model running locally, the possibilities are endless. Think about a:</p><ul><li><p><strong>A baby monitor</strong> that alerts you only if your baby rolls into an unsafe position.</p></li><li><p><strong>A pet cam</strong> that logs what your dog is up to all day.</p></li><li><p><strong>A security camera</strong> that only notifies you when it sees a person, not a stray cat or a blowing leaf.</p></li><li><p><strong>Focus monitor</strong> that checks if you're actually working or just scrolling on your phone.</p></li></ul><p>All of these ideas are now possible to build for free, with total privacy. You can even <a href="/2026/04/gemma-4-explained-pricing-limits-and.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">fine-tune the model for highly specific tasks</a> by providing your own data, making it even more powerful.</p><h2>What This Means for the Future</h2><p>Having a powerful AI that runs on your personal computer is a massive shift.
