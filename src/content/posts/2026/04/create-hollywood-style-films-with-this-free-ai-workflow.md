Let's walk through the exact six steps to get this running on your own computer.</p><h2>Key Takeaways</h2><ul><li><p>You can automate the entire filmmaking process, from writing the script to animating the scenes, using a chain of AI tools.</p></li><li><p>Maintaining character consistency is possible by generating a character reference sheet and feeding it into the image generation process.</p></li><li><p>Browser extensions can automate repetitive tasks, like generating 20 unique scenes or animating them with specific camera movements, with a single click.</p></li><li><p>This workflow allows for the creation of unlimited, high-quality cinematic videos at no cost.</p></li></ul><h2>Step 1: Write Your Hollywood Story with ChatGPT</h2><p>Every great film starts with a great story. For this workflow, ChatGPT will be your personal screenwriter. Using a specific prompt, you can generate a complete story in seconds—complete with a protagonist, antagonist, plot twists, and a climax.</p><p>After generating the main story, a second prompt will break it down into about 20 distinct scene descriptions. These descriptions are the production blueprints for the entire project. A final prompt helps create detailed character profiles. With these three outputs, you have everything the automation needs to get started.</p><h2>Step 2: Build Consistent Characters in Google Flow</h2><p>One of the biggest challenges in AI video is keeping characters looking the same from scene to scene. The secret is using Google Flow with the Nano Banana 2 model. This new model produces photorealistic, film-quality character sheets.</p><p>You'll take your character descriptions from ChatGPT and paste them into Google Flow. The AI will generate a full character sheet showing a front view, side profile, action poses, and various expressions. This sheet acts as a visual anchor, ensuring your hero looks like your hero in every single shot.</p><h2>Step 3: Auto-Generate All 20 Scenes</h2><p>This is where the bulk automation truly shines. Using a Chrome extension called Autovaleo for Google Flow, you can generate all your scenes automatically. You simply switch the tool to "image to image" mode and upload your character sheets.</p><p>Next, copy all 20 scene descriptions from ChatGPT and paste them into the extension's prompt box. The tool queues up each description as a separate job. With one click, it begins generating every scene, one by one, while you sit back and watch. This level of automation, where a complex process is streamlined, is becoming more common in tech. It's similar in spirit to how platforms simplify big data processing. If you're interested in how powerful engines drive such systems, you can <a href="/2026/03/guide-to-databricks-success.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Master Apache Spark: Your Guide to Databricks Success</a>.</p><h2>Step 4: Animate Every Scene with Meta AI</h2><p>With your still images ready, it's time to add motion. Using Meta AI and another automation extension, you can bring each scene to life. First, you'll use ChatGPT to generate a list of camera movements that match the mood of each scene—like "handheld shake" for an action sequence or "slow dolly in" for a dramatic moment.</p><p>Upload your 20 images to the Meta AI automation tool and paste in the list of camera movements. The extension automatically pairs each image with its corresponding motion prompt. Hit start, and the tool will generate and download all 20 animated clips for you.</p><h2>Step 5: Generate a Cinematic Voiceover</h2><p>Your film needs a voice. Using Google AI Studio's text-to-speech tool, you can create a professional-sounding narrator. Copy your full story text from Step 1 and paste it into the tool. The key here is the "style instructions" field. By adding a simple instruction for a cinematic narrator performance, you can transform a flat text reading into a compelling voiceover.</p><h2>Step 6: Assemble Everything in CapCut</h2><p>With all your assets created, it's time for the final edit. Open a video editor like CapCut and import your voiceover and all 20 animated clips. Place the voiceover on the audio track and arrange the clips on the timeline in order.</p><p>Trim each clip to match the narration, ensuring the visuals align with the story. Add a fitting soundtrack at a low volume to support the mood, use clean cuts between scenes, and add captions for key lines. Export your video, and you're done!</p><h2>The Future of AI Filmmaking</h2><p>This workflow represents a major shift in content creation. What once required a studio and a massive budget can now be accomplished by a single person with a computer. It puts the power of filmmaking into the hands of anyone with a story to tell. As these tools become even more powerful and accessible, we are entering an era where creativity is the only limit. The ability to produce high-quality, automated stories on demand will change entertainment forever.</p>
