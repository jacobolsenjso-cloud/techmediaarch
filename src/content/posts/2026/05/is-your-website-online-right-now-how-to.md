It checks your site from different locations around the world at set intervals, usually every few minutes.</p><p>If it gets a normal response, all is well. You provide UptimeRobot with your website's URL. The service then sends a request (a "ping") to your site every few minutes. If your site responds correctly, the check is marked as "Up." If it fails, it immediately triggers an alert.</p><h3>Setting Up Your First Monitor</h3><p>Getting started takes less than five minutes.
