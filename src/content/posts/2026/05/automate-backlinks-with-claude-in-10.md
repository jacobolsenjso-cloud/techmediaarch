<p>Tired of spending hours on <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=backlink+research&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">backlink research</a>? This guide shows you how to use <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=Claude+AI+tool&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">Claude, an AI tool</a>, to automate the entire process in just 10 minutes. Tools like <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=Ahrefs+SEO+tool&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">Ahrefs</a> or SEMrush show you a website's overall <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=define+domain+rating+SEO&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">domain rating</a> and the total number of backlinks. For example, a site might have 2,000 backlinks, but that doesn't tell the whole story.</p><h3>Key Takeaways</h3><ul><li><p><strong>Do-follow vs. No-follow:</strong> Only "<a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=define+do-follow+links&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">do-follow</a>" links pass SEO credit. "No-follow" links don't.</p></li><li><p><strong>One Link Per Domain:</strong> Google values one link from a domain more than multiple links from the same domain, especially if they come from less important areas like footers.</p></li><li><p><strong><a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=what+are+high-quality+backlinks&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">High-Quality Backlinks</a>:</strong> These are links from sites with a domain rating of 40+ that also get at least 2,000 monthly organic search visitors.</p></li></ul><h3>Filtering for Quality</h3><p>To get a real picture, we need to filter these backlinks. Focusing only on "do-follow" links and considering only one link per domain significantly reduces the number. For instance, 2,000 backlinks might drop to around 400 when these filters are applied.</p><p>Applying further filters for high-quality backlinks—meaning the referring domain has a DR of 40 or higher and gets at least 2,000 monthly organic searches—can narrow it down even further. In one example, a site with 2,000 backlinks was found to have only 31 high-quality backlinks.</p><p>This shows that instead of competing against thousands of links, you're really up against a smaller, more manageable number of high-quality ones.</p><h2>Automating with Claude</h2><img alt="Robot arm automating digital links with glowing network background." height="225" src="/images/c149ea5b92db13ce.jpg" width="400" /><p>This is where AI tools like Claude come in. By using a specific prompt, Claude can act as your senior SEO analyst and automate this entire research process.</p><h3>The Claude Prompt</h3><p>The prompt instructs Claude to access tools like Ahrefs or SEMrush (via Chrome access if needed) and perform a <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=define+backlink+gap+analysis&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">backlink gap analysis</a> for a specific keyword. It's designed to:</p><ul><li><p>Identify the top 10 organic results, skipping ads and irrelevant sites (like YouTube videos).</p></li><li><p>Extract data for each competitor, focusing on do-follow links and one link per domain.</p></li><li><p>Sort competitors by their Domain Rating (DR).</p></li><li><p>Categorize competitor backlinks into tiers:</p><ul><li><p>DR 70+ (Top Tier)</p></li><li><p>DR 50-69 (Mid Authority)</p></li><li><p>DR 30-49 (Low-Mid Authority)</p></li><li><p>DR 0-29 (Low Value/<a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=define+PBNs+SEO&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">PBNs</a>)</p></li></ul></li></ul><p>This categorization provides a clear target for the number of links needed in each tier, rather than just a total number.</p><h2>Analyzing Claude's Output</h2><p>When you run this prompt, Claude can generate a detailed report. For example, in a "<a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=roof+replacement+Austin&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">roof replacement Austin</a>" analysis, Claude might identify:</p><ul><li><p>Competitors using PBNs and <a data-preview="" href="https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&amp;q=define+black+hat+networks+SEO&amp;bbid=427886895467585040&amp;bpid=5976585536547653759" target="_blank">black hat networks</a>.</p></li><li><p>The aggregate number of backlinks in each DR tier for competitors.</p></li><li><p>Average numbers for lower-tier links (DR 0-29).</p></li></ul><p>It's important to remember that while AI provides the data and initial analysis, human SEO experience is still needed. For instance, one high-quality DR 70+ link is often more valuable than several lower-tier links.</p><h3>Time Savings</h3><p>Manually performing this detailed analysis can take an SEO professional one to two days. Claude, however, can generate a similar, detailed strategy in about 10 to 15 minutes. This allows you or your SEO team to review, refine, and execute the strategy much faster.</p><p>This automated backlink research process, combined with an SEO tool and Claude, can significantly speed up your off-page SEO efforts, helping you rank higher and attract more business.</p><script type="application/ld+json">
