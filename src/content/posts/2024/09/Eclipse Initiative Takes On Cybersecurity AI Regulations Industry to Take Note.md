faq:
  - q: What are cybersecurity regulations?
    a: Cybersecurity regulations refer to legal requirements and frameworks that set standards for secure software development and push organizations to adopt robust protection measures. These rules help address modern security threats and ensure compliance across different digital platforms.
  - q: Why must organisations follow cybersecurity regulations?
    a: Organizations must follow these regulations because laws like the European Union's Cyber Resilience Act highlight the urgent need for improved cybersecurity practices and compliance. Adhering to these standards ensures that software remains reliable, secure, and capable of meeting complex legal requirements.
  - q: What is the significance of compliance with cybersecurity regulations?
    a: Compliance is crucial because it helps bridge the gap between government rules and the practical development methods used by open-source communities. Meeting these regulatory demands maintains the integrity and security of software while supporting safe digital transformations.
  - q: What is the Open Regulatory Compliance Working Group?
    a: The Open Regulatory Compliance Working Group is an entity created by the Eclipse Foundation to connect regulatory authorities with the open-source community. Its main purpose is to make sure that open-source software meets AI regulations and other legal requirements effectively.
  - q: How does the Eclipse Foundation approach software security and regulations?
    a: The Eclipse Foundation unites various open-source communities and major companies to establish common specifications for secure software development. Through collaborative initiatives, they aim to enhance software reliability and address pressing challenges related to new digital regulations.
