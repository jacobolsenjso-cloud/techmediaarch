<h2>1. DJI Neo</h2>
<p>The DJI Neo, <a href="https://www.dji.com/newsroom/news/dji-wins-over-20-awards-at-ifa-2024" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">honored with 19 awards</a> at IFA 2024, is DJI's latest innovation in the drone market. This <a href="https://www.dji.com/newsroom/news/dji-ifa-2024-us" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">palm-sized drone</a> is designed for everyday use, making it perfect for vloggers and hobbyists alike.</p><p>Weighing just 135 grams, it can easily fit in a purse or pocket, making it incredibly portable.</p>
<p>The Neo captures stunning 4K stabilized video and offers up to 18 minutes of flight time. It features <a href="/2024/08/the-ultimate-review-of-ai-gadgets.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">AI subject tracking</a> and six QuickShot modes, allowing users to record dynamic footage effortlessly.</p><p>The drone can be controlled via the DJI Fly app or a remote, providing flexible options for different users.</p>
<p>Starting at just $200, the base model includes the drone and a single battery. For those needing more flight time, a bundle with two extra batteries is available for around $280.</p><p>The Neo is compatible with some of DJI's recent controllers and headsets, enhancing the overall user experience.</p>
<blockquote>
The DJI Neo is set to become a favorite for both vloggers and nosy neighbors, thanks to its compact size and advanced features.
</blockquote>
<h2>2. Lenovo Auto Twist AI PC</h2>
<p>Lenovo's Auto Twist AI PC is a <a href="https://www.laptopmag.com/laptops/windows-laptops/lenovos-clever-twist-on-a-2-in-1-laptop-at-ifa-2024-has-wide-ranging-potential" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">clever twist</a> on the traditional 2-in-1 laptop. Debuting at IFA 2024, this concept laptop features <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2024/9/5/24235794/lenovo-auto-twist-ai-pc-concept-ifa-2024" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">a motorized hinge that can unfold itself and turn to face you</a>.</p><p>The hinge responds to voice commands and can track your movements, ensuring the screen is always at the optimal angle.</p>
<p>The laptop's "Dual Degree of Freedom" automatic rotation allows it to switch between modes, including closed, open, and tablet modes. This feature not only enhances usability but also helps reduce eye strain by keeping the screen at the right angle.</p>
<blockquote>
The Lenovo Auto Twist AI PC is one of the more innovative laptop concepts seen in years, showcasing how AI can transform everyday gadgets.
</blockquote>
<p>Additionally, the lid will automatically close when it detects that you've stepped away, adding an extra layer of security and privacy. This makes it a standout device in the realm of AI-powered gadgets.</p>
